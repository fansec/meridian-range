## What the maintainer does

- Decides what lands on `main`.
- Reviews issues and pull requests. Please open an issue before a large change; see
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](./docs/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Keeps the range running only on its isolated lab VM, with the sealed network as the default.
